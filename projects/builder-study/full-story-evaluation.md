# Full construction revision

## Scope and review instructions

Apply the [single live brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md) throughout
Builder: movable workpieces, literal connected parts, visible additions, explicit
processes, short sequential goals, white pages and a lighter material palette.
Rethink unsuitable constructions before producing artwork. The new broad gantry,
electric carousel, tabletop pneumatic claw and compact flying lantern replace
long or weakly compositional passages. Only the final lift tile is fictional.

Physical reviewers inspect actual evaluated Blender meshes, ownership, insertion
paths, supports, retention and full useful motion ranges. They distinguish rigid
geometry and explicitly prescribed snap flex from material simulation, forces or
manufacturing qualification. A final assembled picture alone is insufficient.
Revised candidates receive fresh reviewers; authors' self-checks are supporting
inputs, not independent acceptance.

Visual reviewers inspect loose supplies, intermediate additions, explicit turns,
completed objects and later reuse at playing sizes. They look for concealed
additions, changed dimensions, disconnected supplies, inaccessible pickup areas
and misleading reference pictures. Caption auditors receive only actual pictures
and proposed `{...}` descriptions, without model internals or recipes. Reject
claims about hidden fittings, function, motion, measurements or future steps
that the picture itself does not show.

Logical players receive the ordinary fast/detailed interface and the stated
construction-toy intent. Audited picture descriptions are verbal proxies for
visible art; they do not turn text trials into visual, touch or child playtests.
Browser verification uses actual pointer events and phone touch emulation,
reloading at chapter boundaries. Verification packets, geometry dumps, raw
reviewer outputs and captures remain disposable under the
[retention policy](../../docs/engineering.md#verification-records).

## Logical iteration

The [v38 fast screen](playtests/2026.11.08_00.27.10/report.md) completed all
27 playable chapters with one fresh player per chapter. This was a screening
run, not a three-player synthesis. It found clear guided completion but mostly
low or medium hook, repetitive short result names and limited deliberation.
Its text interface could not show the visible construction changes. Retain that
negative evidence without claiming that captions or attractive art establish
enjoyment. The user's positive experience of the opening is human evidence for
that opening, not for the revised later machines.

Subsequent physical and picture reviews added real access steps: the chassis
arrives upright before underside work; the periscope turns to expose each mirror
opening; the dragon turns for its rear collar. No operation exists merely to
test an object, place it in scenery or connect unrelated toys to the finale.

intersecting parts and concealed additions. Corrections include real receiving

preserved wire routing. Addition checks compare evaluated retained geometry and
materials across every authored fitting; those checks alone cannot prove a
reachable assembly path.

passages and automatically acknowledge hidden-only notices with real replayable
dismiss actions. Reading badges and encyclopedia entries use the filtered text.

## Captioned playtests and naming iteration

The full [v39 fast screen](playtests/2026.11.08_02.48.31/report.md) and
[v39 detailed screen](playtests/2026.11.08_02.48.37/report.md) each completed all
30 playable chapters, with one fresh player per chapter, independent grading and
chapter synthesis. All 60 assignments completed without technical interruptions.
These are chapter screens, not three-player comparisons or cumulative human play.

The fast synthesis credited 85 of 200 actions as worthwhile, with 25 medium,
two high and three low hooks. It does not measure fun. The detailed synthesis
credited 97 of 200 as worthwhile and one of 200 as meaningful deliberation, with
26 medium, three high and one low hook. These are attributed model judgments;
they neither measure children's enjoyment nor demonstrate that captions caused
an improvement over the earlier different manuscript.

Players valued recognizable structures emerging, the projector joining its motor
through the belt, and the finished supports. Repeated fittings and turns were less
novel in text, but generally remained clear and purposeful. Desire to see the
mechanisms operate remains a limitation of this still-art phase. Preserve the
user's requested instruction-led making rather than inserting guessing or prose
to increase decision-density scores.

The detailed screen identified actual naming friction: the final workpiece
alternated between Frame and Whale carrier during fittings and turns. In v40 it
stays **Frame** until the first whale panel, then **Whale** throughout. One matching
goal noun changes too. Chapters 1–26 are byte-identical; all recipes, 54 carrier
PLAN entries, geometry-producing functions and cameras are identical. Every image
is byte-identical. The production record preserves the render-source hashes and
records this later authoring-only source change separately.

Fresh [v40 fast](playtests/2026.11.08_03.13.39/report.md) and
[v40 detailed](playtests/2026.11.08_03.13.40/report.md) screens repeated chapters
27–30 with new participants. All eight assignments completed without technical
interruptions; no naming friction was reported. Fast synthesis credited 13/30
worthwhile actions with four medium hooks. Detailed synthesis credited 14/30
worthwhile actions, zero meaningful deliberation, three medium hooks and one low.
The remaining reservations concern repetition in text and wanting to see the
finished mechanisms operate. Keep those findings alongside the clear construction
payoffs; they do not justify adding guesswork or undertaking the deferred animation
phase. Adopt v40 for this static, guided-construction phase, with child playtesting
and native touch evaluation still outstanding.

After the naming correction, the final v40 compiled game passed the complete
200-action 390×664 touch replay again. Its exhaustive graph retains the same
2,025 states/7,084 edges; live lint retains only the same analysis-limit diagnostic.
Regeneration reproduces the frozen v40 manuscript exactly, and all 454 published
assets plus 47 current production-source hashes match the selected-art record.
