**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 16: Syringes and a hose

Worthwhile: 1.3/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: 0/7 meaningful decisions (average across 3 players)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Strongest moments: Connecting the hose-equipped hand syringe to the claw rewarded all three players with coordinated jaw, linkage and syringe motion, clarifying the assembly and supporting interest in another working mechanism. ## Chapter 25: An open frame

Worthwhile: 2/8 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: 0/8 meaningful decisions (average across 3 players)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Adding the crossbar to the uprights produced a recognizable rectangular frame that all three explicitly valued. Connecting the lead also left all three mildly curious about what the window does, although this was an unresolved-function hook rather than a satisfying mechanical demonstration.
