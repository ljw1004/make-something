# Builder: guided construction

Develop [A Fair in the Air](../../stories/builder/builder.md) under its
[single design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md). Earlier work remains
in the [Builder study archive](../../archive/2026.11.07%20builder%20study/README.md).

The revised story builds independent paper and wooden toys, moving mechanisms,
optical toys and a compact flying lantern around a returning projector. All
workpieces are movable. Literal repeated parts, explicit Fold/Flip/Roll/Punch
processes, short current instructions and picture-only references guide the work
without routine popups. Flat honey wood, lighter green and red, cream and charcoal
sit on white pages. Only the final lift tile is fictional. Animation remains a
separate phase.

Experience hypothesis: following the goal picture and one short current instruction
lets the player see and understand every physical change. Reject fitted parts that
cannot be distinguished, processes that silently add hardware, attachments that
deform unrelated components, or clutter that makes the next piece inaccessible.
Some recoverable tabletop overlap is intentional.

The [full revision record](full-story-evaluation.md) preserves review instructions,
rejected candidates, accepted outcomes and evidence limits. Earlier
[pinwheel](chapter-one-evaluation.md) and [racer](racer-evaluation.md) records remain
attributed to their own versions. Numbered drafts and actual structured playtests
are retained. The selected full candidate is [v40](builder-v40.md); draft artwork
references point to promoted live assets and are not
immutable visual snapshots.

Production sources are in `chapter-one-art/`, `racer-art/` and
[full-story/](full-story/README.md), sharing `materials.py`. Canonical dimensioned
Blender geometry supplies all related stages. `full-story/captions.json` contains
independently audited picture descriptions: `{...}` passages appear in text
players and are hidden in the web game. These verbal proxies do not establish
visual enjoyment or native touch quality.

Selected runtime artwork has one home under `stories/builder/assets/`. The
[selected-art record](runtime-art.json) binds promoted images to source hashes and
render passes. Temporary renders, captures and reviewer inputs belong in ignored
`runs/`; retain only concise outcomes and the standing playtest exceptions.
