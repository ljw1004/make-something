# Builder: movable construction prototype

Refine the opening pinwheel-and-racer sequence of [A Fair in the Air](../../stories/builder/builder.md), governed by its [single design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md). Earlier work remains in the [Builder study archive](../../archive/2026.11.07%20builder%20study/README.md).

The prototype tests a readable movable workpiece, separate Fold and Flip process targets, visible additive fittings, and quiet step-by-step construction. Broad opaque paper uses about half the page width; finished toys remain generously sized, including the pinwheel carried into the racer chapter. Held pieces follow the finger beyond page edges and fit back inside on release. Nine operations take prepared paper through four visible corner folds, a retaining hub, a rear view, axle, handle, and a final front view. Chapter 2 now has two separate bearing-tube fittings in either order. Short labels, matching instructions, white backgrounds and picture-only references apply across Builder; other construction graphs remain the preceding edition. Animation remains deferred.

Experience hypothesis: following the goal picture and one short current instruction lets the player see and understand every physical change, without interruption by result readings. Reject the prototype if fitted parts cannot be distinguished, a process silently adds hardware, an attachment bends the paper, or large artwork prevents reaching the next piece or action.

The [evaluation record](chapter-one-evaluation.md) preserves review instructions and concise outcomes. [v32](builder-v32.md) is the current study preview using promoted live artwork; earlier drafts and playtests remain identified as their own versions.

Production sources are in `chapter-one-art/` and `racer-art/`. Selected runtime art has one home under `stories/builder/assets/`; the old candidate drafts are retained source inputs, not maintained player routes. The [selected-art record](runtime-art.json) binds the promoted images to the reviewed candidate. Temporary renders, browser captures and reviewer packets belong in ignored `runs/builder-chapter-one/`. Verification retains only instructions, concise outcomes and the standing playtest exceptions.
