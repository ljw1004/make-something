# Builder: movable construction prototype

Refine the opening of [A Fair in the Air](../../stories/builder/builder.md), governed by its [single design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md). Earlier work remains in the [Builder study archive](../../archive/2026.11.07%20builder%20study/README.md).

The opening now makes an independent pinwheel, a sail assembly, then a wheeled chassis which receives that sail. All workpieces are movable. Real Fold and Flip processes, individual repeated fittings, short current instructions and picture-only references guide the work without routine popups. The new material palette uses honey wood, forest green, brick red and cream/charcoal on white pages, with simple untextured geometry. Later chapters still await the same construction and visual treatment. Animation remains deferred.

Experience hypothesis: following the goal picture and one short current instruction lets the player see and understand every physical change. Reject a revision if fitted parts cannot be distinguished, a process silently adds hardware, an attachment deforms an unrelated part, or crowded artwork prevents reaching the next piece or process.

The [pinwheel evaluation record](chapter-one-evaluation.md) and [racer revision record](racer-evaluation.md) preserve review instructions and concise outcomes. The selected preview is [v36](builder-v36.md); older drafts and actual playtests remain identified as their own versions. Preview manuscripts refer to promoted live artwork and are not immutable visual snapshots.

Production sources are in `chapter-one-art/` and `racer-art/`, sharing the flat palette in `materials.py`. Selected runtime art has one home under `stories/builder/assets/`. The [selected-art record](runtime-art.json) binds promoted images to their sources. Temporary renders, browser captures and reviewer inputs belong in ignored `runs/`; retain only concise outcomes and the standing playtest exceptions.
