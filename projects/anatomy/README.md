# Anatomy

A combination anatomy and physiology textbook for **grades 9–10, non-AP/non-Honors**. Build cells, tissues, organs and systems, then discover how their relationships sustain a living body. The selected art direction is a naturalistic **1800s anatomical atlas**, with modern explanatory scale and blank paper backgrounds. This remains an unpublished prototype.

The sole manuscript is [anatomy.md](anatomy.md): **132 combination/fitting actions on its native completion route, across 28 playable chapters and an epilogue**. Chapters 1–27 build local anatomy; chapter 28 connects the completed structures in ten operations. All 257 item identities and 23 fitted layers have artwork. [Play the entire illustrated story](http://127.0.0.1:5173/make-something.html?story=projects/anatomy/anatomy.md), or inspect the [chapter sheets, complete icon inventory and combination sequences](http://127.0.0.1:5173/projects/anatomy/atlas/review.html).

Artwork depicts what each thing physically is now. It carries no reagent-history badges, future-purpose symbols, detached enlargements or duplicate organs. The revised opening distinguishes an empty membrane from contained water and uses a porous nuclear envelope. The single completed cell carries into differentiation; the arm chapter first differentiates a bone-building cell, then builds living bone tissue, then humerus, ulna, radius and wrist/hand before biceps and triceps. Hearing and balance finish as local organs. Digestion uses separate enzyme substances and a physically mixed saliva. The [design brief](design-brief.md) owns these rules; [atlas production](atlas/README.md) records implementation and verification, and the [curriculum audit](curriculum.md) records the taught vocabulary.

Earlier text-only baseline, revision and two follow-up experiments completed **114 fresh Terra Medium chapter playthroughs**, with independent grading and verified reports. Results are mixed: chapter 5 retained 2/4 Fun and 4/4 Worthwhile with high detailed hooks; the colon and final assembly improved their detailed profiles. Chapter 4's second mineral fitting remained repetitive, and a feedback-only experiment reduced Fun and was rejected. The [score table](working-notes.md#textbook-spread-audit) retains the decreases and differing action counts. These are scoped model judgments with planned pictures, not a student or cumulative human-play study.

Current verification belongs to the [atlas production record](atlas/README.md). The [independent composition loop](atlas/composition-review/README.md) reviews every chapter’s physical components, actual recipes and resulting anatomy, then sends repairs to fresh reviewers. The earlier model trials below used prior recipes and planned pictures; their scores do not validate the revised illustrated edition.
