## Comparable choices

Each then chose amino acids at step 2 and combined the blood-borne nutrients at step 3 before beginning fat at step 4. Player 2 explicitly wanted to compare the two blood-bound nutrients; the others also followed their shared goal. All expected the parallel and later described the separate crossings as repetitive. The agreement is consistent with the graders' ordering-preference assessments, not proof that identical choices cannot involve inference; the supplied menu and reasons do not show a distinct hypothesis here.

At the fat-cell stage, all three chose packaging at step 5 rather than preparing the lacteal, then used the lacteal at step 6. They expected packaging to precede lymph entry; Player 3 was nevertheless surprised by fat rebuilding, while Players 1 and 2 valued the explanation of the particle. This supports a shared story payoff without overturning the independent judgments that the offered sequence was not a meaningful decision.
