## Chapter 17: A body is a habitat

Worthwhile: 3/3 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Wet food remainder + resident gut microbes was a shared, purposeful choice of fermentation over the mucus habitat. All three valued the connection between fibre left by human enzymes and microbial fatty acids and gas. Adding mucus to the body with fermenting contents gave all three a valued final payoff: the planned cutaway shows a barrier and says colon cells can use some microbial fatty acids, even though the action itself was forced.

Weakest moments: Connecting fermenting contents to the body repeated much of the preceding fermentation picture for all three players. Players 1 and 2 nevertheless valued its uptake arrows; player 3 emphasized the repetition. The later explanation says cells use fatty acids but does not explain how. Adding mucus as the only remaining combination limited decision-making, a menu-and-sequence constraint rather than evidence that the biological connection itself felt arbitrary.
