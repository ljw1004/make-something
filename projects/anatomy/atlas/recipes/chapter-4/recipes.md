# 4. Bones first, then muscles

1. Embryonic cells + BMP-2 protein → Bone-building cells — Embryonic cells: preserve, BMP-2 protein: consume.

2. Bone-building cells + Amino acids — protein building blocks → Soft bone tissue — Bone-building cells: transform, Amino acids — protein building blocks: consume.

3. Soft bone tissue + Bone mineral → Bone tissue — Soft bone tissue: transform, Bone mineral: consume.

4. Bone tissue + Shoulder girdle → Shoulder and humerus — Bone tissue: consume, Shoulder girdle: transform.

5. Bone tissue + Shoulder and humerus → Shoulder, humerus and ulna — Bone tissue: consume, Shoulder and humerus: transform.

6. Bone tissue + Shoulder, humerus and ulna → Shoulder and three long bones — Bone tissue: consume, Shoulder, humerus and ulna: transform.

7. Bone tissue + Shoulder and three long bones → Arm and hand bones — Bone tissue: consume, Shoulder and three long bones: transform.

8. Muscle cells + Arm and hand bones → Arm with biceps — Muscle cells: consume, Arm and hand bones: transform.

9. Muscle cells + Arm with biceps → Arm with biceps and triceps — Muscle cells: consume, Arm with biceps: transform.
