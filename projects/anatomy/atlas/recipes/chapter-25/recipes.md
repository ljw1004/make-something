# 25. A small breach

1. Skin segment + Rough edge → Skin with a cut — Skin segment: transform, Rough edge: preserve.

2. Skin with a cut + Platelets at the cut → Skin with a platelet plug — Skin with a cut: transform, Platelets at the cut: consume.

3. Dissolved clotting protein + Clotting enzymes → Clotting protein threads — Dissolved clotting protein: transform, Clotting enzymes: preserve.

4. Skin with a platelet plug + Clotting protein threads → Skin with a reinforced clot — Skin with a platelet plug: transform, Clotting protein threads: consume.
