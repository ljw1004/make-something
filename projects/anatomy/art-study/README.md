# Anatomy artwork comparison

Independent artwork studies for the [Anatomy project](../README.md), governed by its [single design brief](../design-brief.md) and the [icon workflow](../../../docs/icon-guide.md). The user selected the 1800s anatomical atlas. The [complete selected edition](../atlas/README.md) now lives in the canonical story; these comparison samples retain their original four-chapter conditions.

Every direction covers the same four chapters: **1 — An inside of its own**, **4 — Hard bones and pulling muscles**, **8 — Vibration into signals**, and **18 — Breaking down a meal**. Each artist interprets its assigned references independently, without seeing another artist's output. The revised comparison includes **33 item identities, five fitted layers and seven assembled states per style**. Four chapter panels show the starting and completed player views alongside each complete icon family. Inspection thumbnails enlarge isolated details; the player views show their actual relative sizes.

Backgrounds are plain, ruled or subtly textured blank sheets. Anatomy is drawn by the interactive objects and their fitted parts. Within a construction, constituents anticipate the result's material, viewpoint and magnification; purposeful enlargements belong to the object itself. Historical and unconventional drawing styles retain modern textbook/poster hierarchy for grades 9–10.

The user accepted the full-book Star Trek trial as a fair approximation of the icons needed. Its artwork is reused in this comparison, and its [complete illustrated book](farout-star-trek/README.md) remains available. Every comparison play link opens a dedicated `sample.md` that plays the four illustrated chapters consecutively, then ends. Full `anatomy.md` copies remain as production sources; outside Star Trek, those full copies have placeholders in unselected chapters. The selected atlas edition applies later physical-composition corrections throughout the whole book; the comparison samples remain historical alternatives.

## Viewing and evidence

[Open the comparison gallery](http://127.0.0.1:5173/projects/anatomy/art-study/gallery.html) for all thirteen directions, with four complete chapter panels and a consecutive four-chapter playable sample for each. Every panel is available separately from the gallery. The [complete contact sheet](all-themes.jpg) contains all **52 chapter panels**; each theme's sheet is a larger two-by-two arrangement.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root if the local server is not already running. Choose **Play all four chapters**. Normal progression is **cell boundary → bones and muscles → hearing → digestion → ending**; no debugger is needed. These are chapters 1–4 in the sample, corresponding to original chapters 1, 4, 8 and 18 on the sheets. Sample URLs have separate progress from the older full-manuscript links, so an old save does not resume in an unillustrated chapter. These drafts remain outside the published catalogue.

Selected built-in imagegen masters, available original requests and the exact new generation requests remain with each theme. The playful-collage notes disclose four inherited masters whose original request text was not retained. This revision reuses suitable source paintings and mechanically extracts compatible constituents where possible; new generations or edits are retained by the relevant artist. The [sample builder](checks/build-samples.ts) extracts the illustrated chapters, supplies muscle cells directly for the arm, and removes unused later cell fittings. It preserves the selected goals and artwork. Run `node projects/anatomy/art-study/checks/build-samples.ts`, then `node projects/anatomy/art-study/checks/capture.mjs --sample THEME...` to verify continuous progression. The [common assignment](artist-instructions.md), [coverage inventory](checks/comparison-inventory.json), [presentation resolver](checks/comparison-assets.ts), [browser route](checks/capture.mjs), [semantic comparison](checks/verify-semantics.ts) and [sheet compositor](contact-sheets.py) retain reproducible instructions. Temporary browser captures and review montages belong under ignored `runs/` and are removed after consolidation.

The [completed refresh review](../art-review/2026.11.14_22.26.04/report.md) records current coverage, browser and semantic checks.
