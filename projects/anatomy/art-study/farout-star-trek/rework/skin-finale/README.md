# Urinary, skin, renewal and final assembly artwork

This package owns the 59 identities first declared in chapters 22–28, including ten compound fittings. `map.json` is the integration contract; `finale-presentations.json` supplies the smaller chapter-28 presentations of inherited digestive, repaired-skin, lymph and chest items. The epilogue introduces no additional item.

All anatomy is interactive transparent raster artwork. No background belongs to this package. Six selected built-in imagegen masters and their exact requests are in `sources/`: urinary organs and local physiology; skin construction materials; repair/renewal tissues and materials; the complete body model; its removable cyan model covering; and its empty lower regional receiver. The original supplied Star Trek image provides the material and contour reference. The cyan covering is the anatomical model's flexible outer representation, with real anatomical inspection openings; it is not an extra organ system.

Run `uv run --with pillow --with numpy python produce.py` to regenerate the assets and maps. Pillow operations remove faint generated alpha haze, crop inspected sprite bounds, resize material populations, mask source pixels and composite registered parts. They do not draw anatomical substitutes. The master requests' nominal atlas dimensions are descriptive: the tool returned different dimensions, and extraction uses those actual rasters. The renewal atlas uses individually inspected bounds rather than assuming a perfectly regular grid.

The skin is planned backward from one 800×650 registered construction. Surface cells, lipid seams, deeper fibres, vessels, gland and nerves retain their sizes through construction. Every wound state starts from that same assembled image; only the local breach changes. The three repair fittings independently add the surface, supporting matrix and connected capillary. The outer-forearm locator stays at the same location. The kidney pair, ureters and bladder are disjoint masks of one completed urinary master; their fitted sum restores that master. Enlarged filter/tubule views carry kidney locators, and the gut receiver keeps its intact lining above the blood vessel.

The finale is composed internally at 800×1600 and exported at 0.625 scale. It reuses the sibling packages' actual digestive tract, equipped chest and equipped neural anatomy, alongside this package's urinary organs and supplied lower-body walls and legs. The repaired skin remains a small patch with an enlarged inspection inset. The full body, its covering and all registered fitting canvases share the same export transformation. Production dependencies are relative sibling paths, never files outside the repository.

The final chest uses a 500×470 canvas so the supplied opposite arm and the fitted studied arm retain their full-body size. `chapter14-presentations.json` provides the same geometry at exactly twice that size, with all four overlays and loose rib, intercostal muscle, diaphragm and heart/lung inputs. The early base omits the opposite arm, which is supplied only in the finale. This supplementary handoff was requested during integration because the engine uses one global slot-art registration. It leaves identity ownership with the circulation package and supplies a compatible smaller final-chapter base. The small initial 298×220 chest-only canvas is embedded at (102,0); neither arm is compressed into that thorax rectangle. A framed inspection inset of the actual studied bent arm accompanies the straight-arm supply, fitted overlay and final body, making the model-pose handoff explicit.

The reviewed repair keeps the wound on a red vascular branch, with platelet and fibrin pixels at its severed ends. The registered capillary fitting reuses that network's actual branch pixels to reconnect its matching endpoints; supporting matrix remains above the temporary clot until the repaired result clears it. The small installed skin patch excludes the whole-arm locator; only its larger inspection inset retains that locator. Final torso joins preserve shoulder boundaries and use actual supplied supporting-tissue pixels behind the substituted chest, avoiding rectangular missing areas.

The heart/lung fitting now consumes the circulation package's `heart-pulmonary-connected-reference.png` as one intact organ group. Both pulmonary arteries and the paired returning veins remain attached to the same heart and lung meshes; the compositor never crops out a separate central heart. Existing airway and air-sac pixels surround that group. This source change preserves the established chest canvases, slot coordinates and body placements.
