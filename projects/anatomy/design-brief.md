# Anatomy — active design brief


## Selected artwork: 1800s anatomical atlas

The selected direction is naturalistic 1800s anatomical engraving: believable tissue and bone, fine contours and hatching, restrained washes, and generous specimen sizes. Keep the modern explanatory arrangement and deliberate illustrative scale of the strongest comparison. The whole canonical [story](anatomy.md) must be playable with complete artwork for every supply, intermediate, receiver, fitted layer and final result. The finale uses a taller blank sheet to accommodate its large regional components and readable goals at the shared body scale. The thirteen-style comparison remains supporting material; production lives in [atlas/](atlas/production-brief.md).

Backgrounds are blank drawing surfaces: plain, ruled or lightly textured. All anatomy belongs to the interactive objects and fitted layers. Never pre-paint the lesson into the background.

**Draw what the thing physically IS now—not what it used to be, what consumes it, or what its purpose will be.** A result contains a reagent only when that material physically remains in it. No recipe badges, purpose symbols, organ locators, directional arrows, future ghosts, detached enlargements or miniature process diagrams belong to an object. A named enzyme is the enzyme molecule alone; bile is liquid; fat droplets are droplets. Let play reveal function and material structure suggest possible connections. Explain invisible mechanisms in the prose. Follow the reusable [present-state artwork rule](../../docs/icon-guide.md#depict-the-present-physical-object).

Plan each assembled specimen and its constituent family together. Keep material, geometry, viewpoint and magnification compatible through a construction. Illustrative enlargement may establish a useful viewing scale for a chapter, but a fitted part must join the one continuous specimen: never add a detached zoom to keep it large. Continuous cutaways are welcome. Whole completed structures may be supplied at the next chapter's scale through an explicit handoff, without charging an empty combination to change scale.

The opening membrane is a closed, slightly collapsed enclosure; amorphous water takes its rounded boundary from that enclosure. Preserve tangled DNA and use an anatomically distinctive double-walled, porous nuclear envelope. Build bone tissue from cells and matrix before mineralization, then build the arm's bones and its two muscles. Hearing and balance end with local sensory anatomy; final assembly connects organs into a body. Digestion uses separate concrete enzyme substances and a physically mixed saliva, with no substrate or purpose badges.

The user authorized whole-book generation, graphical verification and the accompanying combination changes. This supersedes the earlier text-only scope. Apply these corrections throughout the manuscript, including later reuse of each part.

Use the icon guide's permission for synecdoche deliberately. Chapter 3 begins with the recognizable single completed cell from chapter 2, representing appropriate embryonic descendants; it need not introduce a new three-cell cluster. A single bone-building cell likewise represents a supply. Show numerous cells when their organization matters, such as a tissue, rather than enforcing literal cell counts through every operation. Growth and multiplication may be included in differentiation or tissue formation without separate token-making actions. This does not make all embryonic cells interchangeable: keep the recipient lineage and developmental context explicit.

Carry learned constructions forward. When a later chapter needs a specialized cell type that the player can meaningfully derive using the established differentiation pattern, construct it instead of supplying it without explanation. Chapter 4 therefore begins with BMP-2 helping prepared embryonic mesenchymal descendants develop into bone-building cells, before matrix production and mineralization. Do not turn this into an obligation to construct every unexamined supporting cell; prioritize continuity and a useful biological relationship. Use the icon guide's [composition review loop](../../docs/icon-guide.md#review-the-construction-not-just-the-cutouts) for every chapter and its revised artwork.

## Intent and audience

Discover how a human body is continually made possible by relationships between its parts. Target grades **9–10, non-AP and non-Honors**, taking ordinary school biology. The entry requirement is basic cell and body-system knowledge, not a completed advanced course. Changing scale must change the explanation: organelles cooperate within cells; cells and surrounding material produce tissues; organs share routes and signals; the whole can act, maintain itself and repair some damage.

The current authorized scope is about **200 combinations across 20–30 chapters** in the sole manuscript, [anatomy.md](anatomy.md). The subject has abundant material. The exploration concerns selection, connections and pacing, not whether the body contains enough parts. A redundant step is an opportunity to spend that action on a richer relationship elsewhere.

Treat **Anatomy as a combination anatomy and physiology textbook**, working through its subject chapter by chapter. Begin with cellular structures, then develop tissues, organs and systems for their own anatomical and physiological interest. Build the nervous system, blood, vessels, heart and respiratory apparatus in their own right before the final regional assembly connects their routes to the arm and the rest of the body. The arm is one useful anatomical region, not the purpose for which the rest of the body is introduced. Apply the nervous-system work immediately in vision, hearing and balance, then continue through blood, breathing, defense, nutrition, regulation, skin repair and renewal. Earlier experiments and their removed manuscripts remain historical evidence; all continuing story work belongs in `anatomy.md` under this brief.

Audit each playable chapter as a **textbook spread**: state its central anatomy/physiology question, the coherent chunk it teaches, and the actual illustration that could carry that explanation. Use one continuous anatomical subject or connected physical system. Separate constructions may share the page, but no combined object gains a duplicate inset or process diagram. Different scales must not masquerade as one local cutaway, and a network spanning the body must not fit wholly inside a chest or head. Different organs may share a physiology spread when their relationship is the subject. A good picture plan does not establish finished-art recognition or Fun. Short comparisons must earn their place as payoffs of earlier construction; label the concluding prose as an epilogue. Finish local organ systems before the final regional assembly. Put detailed per-chapter audit findings in the consolidated working notes.

The opening develops **inside and outside; fuel, mitochondria and protein production; differentiation into specialized cells**. The separate division exercise is cut: adding DNA material and then a membrane supply did not make a convincing construction. Division may appear within an actual population change or renewal example without a compulsory molecular assembly chapter. Keep glucose entry with respiration and ATP use. The recognizable “powerhouse of the cell” title may invite interest, but its combinations must reveal what energy transfer accomplishes. Name actual constituents such as cytosol rather than a catch-all “cell contents.” A membrane enclosure alone is not a living cell. Introduce organelles through their work, without requiring a catalogue of parts in the first chapter.

Keep the opening's physical identity coherent. First construct a closed membrane enclosure with an inside and an outside; fit only persistent structures into that compound. Glucose is consumed material, not a fitted component; feeding it to a mitochondria-containing cell can produce a separate representational ATP icon while preserving the cell. Establish oxygen and other necessary background machinery visibly where relevant. There is no ATP-plus-cell → “working cell” operation: the cell was already living. Pumping must occur across an enclosed boundary. Introduce channels and pumps where their function matters, including the later nerve-cell example, rather than requiring unused opening fittings. Completed anatomical wholes may have simple representations, but a renaming or activation badge must not be the reason for another action.

In differentiation, identify the actual substance, its releasing tissue, its recipients and its developmental setting. Naming a messenger after the job we want it to do does not ground it. The selected chapter explicitly steps back to an embryo: an early limb-bud view shows Sonic hedgehog from the zone of polarizing activity acting on selected muscle precursors; a later view shows TGF-beta2 associated with developing cartilage, muscle and tendon cells helping tendon precursors continue developing. State those sources and times in the chapter opening and readings. The supplied proteins depict their own molecular structures; neither background nor item art carries source locators or temporal diagrams. The next chapter explicitly returns to the anatomical model. Neither protein alone converts an arbitrary cell; the recipients' appropriate prior histories, other cues and cellular machinery are supplied. A physical scaffold would be a different example. Intermediate cell names and pictures must identify an actual material change; “young” versus “developing” alone does not earn an operation. Distinguish RNA instructions from proteins made from amino acids, intracellular pulling proteins from whole muscle fibres, and early developmental regulation from its later protein products.

Textbook organization must still support enjoyable construction. The opening should offer independent, understandable things to build, useful ingredients with more than one role, and branches that later meet. Use Creation's opening as a structural reference: informed players can pursue different valid constructions with the normal compatibility information present. A short causal sequence can serve a purpose; an extended opening of compulsory one-option steps does not meet this requirement. Extra inspection, uncertainty or errors do not substitute for variation in what can be built. High worthwhile scores for explanatory results cannot establish that this interaction requirement has been met. The [opening-linearity review](working-notes.md#opening-linearity-review) records the user's rejection of the preceding linear opening; the [current selection](working-notes.md#textbook-spread-audit) records the latest revision and links its preceding evidence.

Convergence is expected. The user explicitly distinguishes choosing between useful independent constructions, `(A+B)+(C+D)`, from merely changing additions to one object, `(A+B)+C` versus `(A+C)+B`. The former can supply Fun through deciding what to work on next, even if all branches are required and reach the same final body. Do not insist on extra uncertainty, experiments, strategic tradeoffs or risk of failure. Apply the [standing distinction](../../docs/story-authoring.md#make-combining-worthwhile) and its corresponding evaluation criterion when judging Anatomy; preserve earlier results under the criteria that produced them.

Make those projects visible in the goals, usually two together and occasionally three. A distant whole-system goal does not invite the independent work as clearly as separate artery, capillary and venous-return goals. Stage dependent work within each project, while leaving the other pursuits visible. Follow [purposeful goals](../../docs/story-authoring.md#write-purposeful-goals-and-continuity); changes to that invitation may affect Fun and require appropriate retesting.

Chapters guide attention and clear away old items; their order is not a reason to forbid an otherwise sound construction. Welcome useful early completion and legitimate shortcuts with already available pieces, following the [standing early-completion guidance](../../docs/story-authoring.md#write-purposeful-goals-and-continuity). Audit duplicate supplies or recipes for their actual contribution and clarity rather than treating a bypass of the intended lesson sequence as a defect by itself. Later supplied parts or new physical contexts may still serve the anatomical progression.

Use a clearly identified embryonic-cell model, including its already established cellular identity, for early differentiation. The reusable population represents distinct descendants with appropriate histories in different developmental scenes, not interchangeable cells from one limb. Later adult renewal has its own tissue-appropriate populations. An arbitrary mature cell must not become pluripotent just by adding a component. DNA replication and DNA-to-RNA-to-protein expression have different purposes. Show differentiation through changes in gene activity, protein production and function; respect lineage restrictions and relevant intermediate stages. Tissue additionally requires organization and extracellular material. The late renewal chapter should apply these earlier ideas in the adult body rather than repeat the entire opening sequence.

Use a consistent level of construction for comparable structures. Teach common tissue organization once, then use it consistently in the named organs: biceps and triceps should both be made from the same level of constituent tissue, rather than building one from cellular components and supplying the other whole. Shared tissue items can represent populations with sufficient material for several anatomical regions; drawings and notes must make this scope explicit. Regional anatomy must still be real tissue and structures, not an empty framework or an “arrangement” token.

The [curriculum audit](curriculum.md) must check actual item titles, scientific terms and required mechanisms against ordinary biology expectations. A syllabus heading such as cell division does not certify all of its molecular machinery. The first two chapters must remain within ordinary school cell and energy ideas, with modest initial demands. AP/Honors curricula are comparison sources for identifying extensions, not the audience baseline. Preserve worthwhile later subjects while rebuilding their prerequisites at the intended level.

Keep liver storage and release in one chapter immediately after absorption; do not restore a slight standalone storage chapter. Keep airway defense beside breathing, immune memory beside lymph, and skin → injury → repair → renewal together before final assembly. The [textbook-spread audit](working-notes.md#textbook-spread-audit) records the current order, kidney/transport identity corrections and their evidence. The earlier human item audit remains historical provenance.

### Local chapters, then final assembly

Organ chapters are anatomy-textbook chapters about their own physical subject. Finish a skin segment, eye, kidney assembly or nerve cell without routinely installing it into an accumulated body. Injury and repair stay on the local skin cross-section. Keep the completed structures available, or explicitly present the same completed structure at the later scale when the engine requires a handoff.

Reserve whole-body assembly for a distinct final phase. Its payoff is bringing the already understood parts together into one recognizable anatomical model, with several useful projects and flexible orders. Restrict order when physical support, a connection or access makes it meaningful; internal organs before an outer covering can be justified, while an arbitrary preferred organ order cannot. This is explanatory model assembly, not surgery or an embryological sequence. Supplied unexamined anatomy must be visible and named honestly. A changed scale or sectional view can hand off an already completed region explicitly: the finale’s Chest region contains the completed diaphragm, heart and lungs, then accepts the arm and lymph return at whole-body scale. This is the same completed anatomy in a new presentation, not another physiological construction or a second set of organs. Do not duplicate a structure when two earlier chapters examined different views of it, or stretch a literal skin sample into an entire body's covering.

A compound must have a reason to exist: its assembled structure enables the next use, completes a meaningful local anatomical subject, or contributes to the final whole. A container named after a collection is not enough. Show the actual boundaries, attachment, continuity or material organization that makes its parts one thing. Blood remains blood when context changes its water content; the setting supplies the condition. A real root-to-spinal-nerve connection is clearer than a generic bundle of incoming and outgoing routes.

Use the digestion chapter as a positive design reference: recognizable substances, independent useful transformations, and material changes that the player can follow. Structural chapters need their own equally concrete contributions. Simplification should remove weak assembly tasks such as a separate elbow-joint fitting, while retaining valuable contrasts such as building biceps and triceps at comparable scales and seeing their opposite pulls.

### Lessons that govern every revision

Read these before drafting, and apply them across the whole manuscript when user feedback reveals a recurring pattern. The user must not have to discover the same defect chapter by chapter. These constraints govern selection before playtest scores are considered; high model scores do not overrule them.

- **Choose one role for each representation.** A structural part must be installed before the containing structure can use it. A molecular icon may represent a substance at an enlarged scale, with its location explicit. Do not let an item silently alternate between loose part, installed component and detached functional close-up. In the opening, ribosomes are installed structures; RNA stays pictured inside the cell, and ATP acts on that cell to produce protein.
- **Handle things.** Every item must name a physical substance, structure, organism or materially distinct state that could be drawn without an explanatory caption rescuing it. “Oxygen entering,” “warming sensed,” “working cell” and “body regulating” are events or accomplishments, not new things. Put processes in the action, pictures and brief wonders. A real molecule such as ATP or a chemical messenger may have a representational icon at a different scale.
- **Keep reusable types distinct from anatomical destinations.** Use one Muscle cells supply for both biceps and triceps; the arrangement and attachment context supplies the difference. Do not manufacture “front muscle cells” and “back muscle cells” to encode the intended recipe before play. One supply represents sufficient material for both constructions, not one literal cell duplicated. The selected arm starts at an actual shoulder girdle. Bone-building cells make collagen-rich soft tissue from amino acids; mineralization produces reusable living bone tissue. Successive additions build humerus, ulna, radius, then the grouped wrist and hand. The first Muscle cells addition builds biceps; the second builds triceps. These are distinct successive anatomical states, not two indistinguishable slots on one owner. The same pattern can later build other named bones or individual muscle layers when each addition earns its action; future examples are not compulsory additions to this edition. A later whole-arm handoff remains explicit because a fixed local region cannot silently become a movable part.
- **Specialize whole cells; do not supply their growth as detachable parts.** A nerve fibre is an extension of a neuron. Reuse the established cell model's recognizable membrane, nucleus and organelles, and show the cell growing its extensions. Local precursor identities may expose the distinct prepared groups within the earlier broad population; identify that handoff rather than charging an empty conversion action. The selected neural example uses spinal-cord precursors with Sonic hedgehog, and Schwann-cell precursors contacting neuregulin-1 on an existing peripheral axon. The same molecule has different effects in different prepared recipients; do not use identical unrestricted cells plus identical protein to imply arbitrary outcomes. The membrane-bound signal remains a fixed view of its source, not a free-floating reagent. Channels fit into the whole neuron's membrane; separate Schwann cells wrap its peripheral extension while keeping their own membranes and nuclei. No detachable axon, generic “nerve program,” catalogue of glia or intracellular cascade is required.
- **Do not turn transport or purpose into a new substance.** Glucose remains glucose when it crosses the gut wall or travels toward the liver; Blood remains Blood near a kidney; urine remains urine when it enters a bladder. Keep destination, provenance and completed actions in location, pictures, goals or prose. Name a new material when separation or chemistry creates a distinct physical output, such as kidney filtrate separated from blood or glucose joined into stored carbohydrate. Inspect the reaction as well as the title: renaming a journey-token does not repair a recipe whose only accomplishment was movement.
- **Separate structures from supplies.** Persistent anatomical parts may occupy compound slots. Glucose, oxygen, amino acids and transient messages arrive, are used or leave. Their presence in a cell does not make them permanent fittings. Preserve producers when they make products: a cell can make ATP, a gland can secrete a substance, and marrow can make new blood cells without becoming a production-status tile.
- **Demand a material contribution.** State what each input contributes and what physically changes. Do not add DNA to a cell as though it had none, re-add an organ already in a body, or combine an observation of a process back into its subject. A real tissue close-up can be a fixed working context; it must remain recognizably part of the existing anatomy, not a duplicate organ to install.
- **Make anatomy earn its actions.** Prefer a short build → use → consequence relationship. The nucleus supplies RNA instructions; carriers admit glucose; mitochondria support ATP production; ribosomes use instructions and material to make proteins. Introduce a membrane channel when its transport or signalling function matters. If a separate fitting contributes only “this also belongs here,” bundle it unless its construction itself is worthwhile.
- **Choose a useful scale.** Anatomical truth alone does not justify every sleeve, lining, salt, cell or intermediate title. A crowded board of weakly distinguished parts makes valid pairings feel arbitrary. Supply ordinary supporting tissue and machinery together, keep comparable structures at comparable scales, and cut weak stages or chapters rather than explain them more elaborately.
- **Respect natural assembly order.** If tendons could attach to bones before muscle tissue is added, a muscle-first recipe must not pretend that is the only anatomical order. Support the useful alternatives, use physical locations where they simplify play, or include tendons in the supplied anatomy. Do not multiply status items merely to encode every permutation. When an organ joins a larger whole, retain its established uses through that whole where the chapter needs them. Explain real prerequisites in current goals rather than relying on a rejected drag to reveal them.
- **Keep recognizable independent projects.** Simplification must retain choices between useful things to build. Merging short chapters can bring a bone project and a muscle project onto one board before they meet. Reordering fittings on a single object is weaker than this choice. Usually show two current goals.
- **Judge the actual school-level demand.** Ordinary ninth- and tenth-graders must understand the choices from titles, pictures and prior play. A curriculum heading, a compatibility hint, or a model's expert explanation does not establish that understanding. Keep essential knowledge visible and prose brief; use author notes for the detailed scientific limits.

For each substantial revision, record the specific requirement, the changed recipes and downstream uses, the explicit cuts/bundles, the source and prerequisite check, and the focused fast/detailed evidence. Look for the same failure in later chapters before delivering the correction. See the standing [authoring workflow](../../docs/story-authoring.md#develop-a-story) for review independence and change propagation.

## Scientific and editorial boundaries

Let structure and function supply the chapter's motivation. Concrete physiological changes can demonstrate a relationship without a recurring external task, character or prop. Do not organize the book around holding a cup, adding a load or completing a reach. The body can be understood as active even while visibly still. A clot and healed skin are different achievements; food in the gut and fuel available to a cell are different locations; nerves carry information while blood carries materials. Homeostasis involves sensing a condition and responses that change it, not merely attaching a tile named “balance.”

System integration follows system construction. A nervous-system model contains the brain and spinal cord in their central positions, with peripheral routes extending outward; it is not a brain added inside an arm. Connecting that system to a region should reveal its actual nerve endings and signal directions. Similarly, circulation reaches a region through arterial, capillary and venous connections after blood, vessels and heart have been developed. Avoid providing a second whole central nervous system or duplicate circulation in a later close-up.

This is an explanatory model. Selected cell division and differentiation processes are now part of the authorized teaching, but the whole assembly is not a complete embryological timeline, surgical operation, manufacture of life from isolated organelles or replay of evolution. Supplied assemblies may include ordinary supporting anatomy. State an omission where it materially affects the current explanation. Model parts must still make the represented relationship true.

ATP is a repeatedly remade carrier supporting cellular work, not energy created from nothing or a permanently full battery. Mitochondria support much aerobic ATP production, not all ATP production in all cells. Food and oxygen have different roles. Avoid fixed ATP yields, proton-gradient bookkeeping, named enzyme chains, nephron segments, hormone lists and other distinctions whose primary effect is increasing the operand count. Cell types differ; a representative animal cell is not a universal list of obligatory parts.

Retain immune-cell activation as a worthwhile subject at the bounded level below. Ordinary GCSE specifications explicitly support antigens, lymphocytes, antibodies and immune memory; the whole subject must not be rejected as advanced merely because its detailed machinery is complex. Connect recognition and activation to the earlier lessons in specialization and protein production; show division where the immune-cell population actually grows. Teach any new names before their use, and distinguish antibody binding from engulfment. Dendritic-cell presentation, named helper-T/B-cell choreography and intracellular signaling are outside the ordinary version. A compact response chapter may be an earned payoff after the independent airway defenses; record its actual Fun rather than inventing branches to inflate it.

Use author-only `> NOTE:` passages in the manuscript to state the grounded truth, why it matters, the connection to human experience/evolution/medical history where useful, and the model's limits. Attach source references to those notes. Historical parallels must not be invented or used as a decorative claim that a recipe reenacts a discovery. Necessary player knowledge belongs in the visible text, not only in notes or optional encyclopedia readings.

## Compound objects and dissection

Use the existing engine. A completed compound can be an explicitly qualified input to an ordinary transformation, including in the same chapter. Its new identity has its own parts and readings; the old slots do not automatically transfer. The other input must contribute something real. Avoid an empty “completion,” “zoom out” or “next scale” combination solely to rename a tile.

The engine has one result per combination and no automatic multi-part disassembly. Dissection remains an exploratory idea, not a requirement. An inspection or prepared-kit abstraction is possible, but must earn its time and make its scope honest. Prefer an intact construction arc when taking things apart adds no new understanding. No engine/UI changes are authorized for this study.
