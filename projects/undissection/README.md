# Undissection

Gameplay development for a combination story about reconstructing a human body and discovering why its parts need one another. Current intended audience: grades 8–10, with basic school biology. This is an active text-only study; there is no artwork or publication work in scope.

**Current result:** *Lift a glass*, a four-chapter organ-and-system prototype with 14 combinations. It connects opposing muscle pulls, sensory control, blood supply, breathing and digestion around one ordinary action. The previous kidney-centred study was rejected as too advanced; this is a new design for grades 8–10.

Two school-version Terra `--fast` campaigns are complete: 24 chapter playthroughs, each independently graded, with eight fresh chapter syntheses. The final chapters received worthwhile counts of **2/3, 3/3, 3/3 and 5/5**, with medium/high, high, medium/high and medium hook judgments respectively. These are small adult-AI samples. Fun was not measured; student understanding and enjoyment remain untested. The strongest ending discovery is that holding the glass still also requires continuing muscle effort. The main limitation is predictable guided assembly. [Findings and limits](working-notes.md#school-revision-outcomes-and-current-judgment).
