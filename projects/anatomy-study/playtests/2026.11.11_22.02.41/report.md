# Report

Play style: --fast

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 1: Inside the muscle

Worthwhile: 5.3/8 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Strongest moments: Fitting ribosomes into the muscle-cell model gave all three players a valued explanation linking copied instructions to protein production. Adding pulling threads produced fibers that all three found satisfying and gave them a reason to consider continuing.

Weakest moments: Making cell fluid from water and mineral salts felt predictable and minimally explanatory to Players 1 and 3; Player 2 also found it predictable. Making nuclei from DNA and nuclear envelopes was Player 2’s least worthwhile moment, although Player 3’s grader credited the choice as meaningful. Repeated fittings were predictable for Player 3, while the missing explanation of how fibers pull was a content gap noted by Players 2 and 3.

## Chapter 2: A pull has a running cost

Worthwhile: 4.7/5 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: high

Strongest moments: Adding oxygen to sugar-processing muscle fibers produced an ATP cycle that all three players identified as their most worthwhile result, connecting energy supply to muscle work.
