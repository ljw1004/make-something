[Full production source](anatomy.md). The full manuscript preserves the original gameplay. Only the four comparison chapters have complete artwork; other identities and fittings use explicit neutral placeholders.

The current version contains all **33 chapter identities and five relevant fitted layers**, including all independent arm fitting combinations. Its plain near-white sheet contains no anatomy, diagrams or labels. Fine teal contours, shaded coral tissue, lilac cellular structures, cyan fluid and orange nutrients/nerve pathways form the common visual language. The anatomical arm, its labels and its connected bone enlargements belong entirely to two complementary interactive receiver layers. Their combined silhouettes produce one arm, with independent mineral and muscle additions.

The opening uses a newly generated matched family. Water is the volume enclosed by the membrane; lipid pieces repeat at the membrane's own scale. DNA, nuclear envelope and assembled nucleus share a smaller scale, and the nucleus retains that scale when fitted. The original selected hearing and digestion family is reused with an explicit ear-size hierarchy. Fat digestion now produces disconnected glycerol and fatty-acid pieces. The original spindle-shaped muscle precursors remain: the manuscript explicitly supplies precursors before they assemble into skeletal muscle, so the new atlas's mature striated fibre sample is not selected.

Run `uv run --with pillow python projects/anatomy-study/art-study/modern-student/prepare.py` from the repository root. The original atlas, scene masters, original requests, registration and former producer remain in `sources/` for provenance. The former backgrounds are not runtime assets.

[sources/registration-refresh.json](sources/registration-refresh.json) records crop bounds, scales and registration.
