# Star Trek whole-book artwork rework

The [Anatomy design brief](../../../../../stories/anatomy/design-brief.md) owns lasting intent. This production pass implements the user's correction to the first comparison: every chapter must be illustrated; backgrounds are blank sheets; the interactive constructions themselves carry the anatomy. Other comparison styles are unchanged pending this trial.

## Shared production contract

- Use the supplied `projects/anatomy-study/inspiration/farout-star-trek.png` as the drawing reference: luminous cyan contours, translucent coral/violet living tissues, ivory skeleton, restrained gold details. Read the imagegen skill and use built-in imagegen. Selected generated raster masters and exact requests are retained. Mechanical extraction, masking and compositing are allowed; do not substitute coded anatomical drawings.
- All chapters share a plain midnight-navy sheet with a very faint technical grid. No background anatomy, organs, explanatory diagrams, finished examples, text or labels. The coordinator supplies this background.
- Illustrate every identity, intermediate state, fixed receiver and compound fitting through chapter 28 and the epilogue. No neutral placeholders, empty visible items, badges or arbitrary decorative symbols.
- Plan families from the completed construction backward. Constituents must look like the actual material or part visible in the result, at compatible scale and viewpoint. Keep shapes, colors, orientation and material throughout a family. A small cluster of giant molecules becoming a tiny organ is not acceptable. For many repeating units, show a population matching the product's unit size and detail. Liquids are spatial volumes or layers, not teardrop symbols; proteins are tangible molecular forms, not badges.
- Show transformations using the same object before/after. Finished-master decomposition and registered compositing are preferred for physical assemblies. When enlargement is needed, integrate an explicit locator or cutaway into the object's own artwork, not the background, and keep it consistent through that chapter family.
- Use transparent PNG artwork. Maximize useful anatomical pixels while preserving margins. Intrinsic artwork is displayed at one-quarter scale on the 390px reference phone. Typical individual parts should be 240–520px wide, assemblies 600–960px, detailed fixed anatomy up to 1200px within the scene. Set the sizes deliberately across a family; do not independently normalize every tiny part to the same large square. All compound overlays use the exact base canvas. Retain partial states without repainting other fittings.
- All pixel positions use a 1560×3376 background. Prefer automatic supply arrangement. Fixed targets generally fit in the upper-middle region, leaving supplies and goals room. Hitbox positions/sizes use scene coordinates; a shared arm can use two separate receiver hitboxes with one registered visible anatomy base.
- Preserve original narrative, identities, supplies, rules, readings, fates and goals. Artwork metadata only. The coordinator integrates the full copy. Do not edit the original manuscript or application.
