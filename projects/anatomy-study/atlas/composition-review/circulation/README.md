# Circulation, lung and chest composition repair

Selected production artwork for chapters 11–14 and 16. The [active design brief](../../../../../contrib/anatomy/design-brief.md) and [icon guide](../../../../../docs/icon-guide.md) govern this package. The original `atlas/circulation` package and canonical manuscript were not overwritten by this producer.

`map.json` supplies 46 item mappings, the two registered chest slots, and the chapter-12 regional presentation of the body vessels. `changes.json` supplies the exact proposed descriptions, approved chapter-13 renames and explicit whole-lung handoff for integration. Run `produce.py` with Pillow and NumPy from any directory. It reads only selected source paintings and performs masks, crops, alpha composition and scale normalization; it does not draw anatomy.

All newly painted sources were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The adjacent `*-request.json` files preserve exact requests and reference-image roles. Each request with references declares `reference_base`, resolved relative to the request file: `.` means the source directory and `..` means the package directory. The exact prompt text is unchanged. Superseded paintings remain only when they are necessary inputs in the selected generation chain, never as alternate runtime choices. `endothelial-sheet.png` reuses the existing selected endothelial depiction from `../../circulation/assets/blood-facing-lining-cells.png`; its original generation is recorded in `../../circulation/sources/vessels-request.json`. No API/CLI image-generation fallback was used.

## Selected repairs

- **11:** A newly painted continuous tissue route replaces a vessel mesh pasted over already vascular tissue. The artery narrows through branches, capillaries occupy one tissue patch, and collecting branches enter the valved vein. Inputs retain the installed scale. The isolated valve is taken from its actual wall-rooted position. The pre-valve vein is painted with an uninterrupted lumen. Smooth lining persists in all later arterial and venous states, including beneath the integrated valve bases.
- **12:** A mostly exterior regional heart preserves a broad convex anterior myocardial surface for coronary branches. Small lateral windows expose ventricular interiors. The right input is a prepared atrium and ventricular free-wall shell with venae cavae; the left input is the matching atrium and thick ventricular core with a medial receiving surface. The prepared internal surfaces are concealed by reunion. The complete open pulmonary vascular preparation is shared across its standalone input, right route and both whole states. A single continuous aorta occupies the posterior plane, with its cut continuations beyond the regional specimen. Sparse coronary trunks and their companion veins follow the intact exterior wall instead of an exposed septum.
- **13:** The construction remains at the scale of one lung lobule. Terminal airway, fine branching tree, alveolar epithelium, capillaries and elastic support are coherent physical constituents. Whole lungs are supplied explicitly in chapter 14. This is an editorial choice to preserve the local airway-to-exchange-surface lesson; representative ingredients and population transitions remain permitted. The baseline reviewer accepted the old macro result, so this choice is not presented as a reviewer-required ban on synecdoche. The repaired front sectional cavities retain clear air spaces; capillaries and fine elastic support follow retained walls.
- **14:** One completed thoracic painting establishes lateral rib/intercostal walls, posterior spine, lungs, heart and shallow domed diaphragm. Generated constituent states share that geometry. The exposed column is corrected to one consistent anterior view of vertebral bodies and discs; the inferior cage remains open before fitting its diaphragm. The final pulmonary connections are painted together, and the lung bases and heart rest on the diaphragm. All fitted layers use one 1026 × 1082 runtime canvas (the 1221 × 1288 master at 0.84 scale).
- **16:** One continuous simplified specimen shows blind-ended tissue lymphatics, collectors, valves and an actual opening into a receiving venous junction. It retains the explicitly simplified/compressed anatomical overview; it does not imply microscopic lymphatics directly adjoin a neck vein at literal physical scale. The vessel-only extraction preserves closed tips and eliminates tissue speckles.

## Verification record

The independent frozen baseline reviews for all five chapters were read. Their central failures—duplicated vessels, abrupt luminal junctions, detached valve appearance, unexplained scale changes, unsupported coronary overlays, fibers crossing cavities, ambiguous chest depth and an invisible lymphatic ostium—guided the coordinated redraws.

Local verification rendered all 22 native required operation strips at the same playing scale using the replacement map, including both chest-slot orders represented by the existing family. Inputs, intermediate identities, installed dimensions and standalone final wholes were inspected. Image files are nonempty RGBA assets; the producer completes without missing source paths. These are production and informed visual checks, not learner-recognition, factual-certification or human-learning evidence. Fresh independent composition reviews accepted all five chapters with no remaining issues. The final exterior-heart family and its downstream chapter-14 handoff were both accepted in round 19. The final continuous browser run completed all 28 chapters and 132 actions, including reload checks and the epilogue. These judgments concern the frozen visual sequence and do not establish learner outcomes or medical certification.

Temporary contact sheets, operation strips and rejected trials live under ignored `runs/anatomy-composition-review/`. They are not permanent evidence deliverables.

The current selected masters are `vascular-lined-valved.png` for the later vessel states, `lobule-duct-v2.png` for the completed lobule, `thorax-anterior-spine.png` and `thorax-anterior-bones.png` for the exposed chest family, and `lymph-master.png` for the lymphatic route. `complete-chest.png` is the selected whole-thorax handoff artifact, retained alongside mapped runtime assets.

For chapter 12, `exterior-cardiac-master.png` supplies the foreground exterior heart. `exterior-bare.png` contributes plain myocardial tissue for the preceding state. Both final states retain the same myocardial contour, lateral windows, shared pulmonary preparation and aorta. `cardiac_family.py` selects the generated tissue contours and establishes the normal posterior-aorta/anterior-pulmonary depth order. The isolated coronary ingredients use one common scale normalization to restore their relationship to the whole heart. These operations are mechanical preparation of painted anatomy.

The `exterior-*-route-guide.png` images are mechanical rough alpha selections of the corresponding generated route references. They communicate the intended removed ventricular portion to the image generator; the selected `exterior-*-route-section.png` sources repaint their section boundaries as anatomical tissue. The guide construction is recorded in `exterior-route-guides-provenance.json`.

The assembled aortic route has one left-ventricular valve/root and a posterior descending limb. The construction reference is [NHLBI, blood flow through the heart](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/blood-flow), supported by [OpenStax, heart anatomy](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/19-1-heart-anatomy). These references establish the anatomy; they do not certify the generated illustration.
