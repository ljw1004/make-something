# 12. A pump with two routes

1. Right side of heart + Lung blood-vessel network → Right heart and lung route — Right side of heart: transform, Lung blood-vessel network: consume.

2. Left side of heart + Body blood-vessel network → Left heart and body route — Left side of heart: transform, Body blood-vessel network: consume.

3. Arteries for the heart muscle + Heart-muscle capillaries and veins → Heart-muscle vessel network — Arteries for the heart muscle: transform, Heart-muscle capillaries and veins: consume.

4. Right heart and lung route + Left heart and body route → Heart and two routes — Right heart and lung route: transform, Left heart and body route: consume.

5. Heart and two routes + Heart-muscle vessel network → Heart with connected vessels — Heart and two routes: transform, Heart-muscle vessel network: consume.
