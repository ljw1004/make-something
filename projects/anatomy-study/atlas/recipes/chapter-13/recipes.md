# 13. Air beside blood

1. Tiny air sacs + Lung capillaries → Air sacs with blood vessels — Tiny air sacs: transform, Lung capillaries: consume.

2. Air sacs with blood vessels + Elastic supporting tissue → Elastic air sacs with blood vessels — Air sacs with blood vessels: transform, Elastic supporting tissue: consume.

3. Terminal airway + Fine branching air tubes → Lobular airways — Terminal airway: transform, Fine branching air tubes: consume.

4. Lobular airways + Elastic air sacs with blood vessels → Lung lobule — Lobular airways: transform, Elastic air sacs with blood vessels: consume.
