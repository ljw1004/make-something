# 7. From light to sight

1. Clear dome (cornea) + Coloured muscle ring (iris) → Cornea and iris — Clear dome (cornea): transform, Coloured muscle ring (iris): consume.

2. Cornea and iris + Clear lens → Light-controlling eye front — Cornea and iris: transform, Clear lens: consume.

3. Light-sensitive cells + Retinal nerve cells → Retina with optic nerve — Light-sensitive cells: transform, Retinal nerve cells: consume.

4. Light-controlling eye front + Retina with optic nerve → Eye with optic nerve — Light-controlling eye front: transform, Retina with optic nerve: consume.
