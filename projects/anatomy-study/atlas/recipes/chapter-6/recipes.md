# 6. Brain, spinal cord and nerves

1. Brain + Skull → Brain inside the skull — Brain: transform, Skull: consume.

2. Spinal cord + Backbone → Spinal cord inside the backbone — Spinal cord: transform, Backbone: consume.

3. Brain inside the skull + Spinal cord inside the backbone → Brain and spinal cord — Brain inside the skull: transform, Spinal cord inside the backbone: consume.

4. Sensory nerve root + Motor nerve root → Spinal nerve — Sensory nerve root: transform, Motor nerve root: consume.

5. Brain and spinal cord + Spinal nerve → Nervous system model — Brain and spinal cord: transform, Spinal nerve: consume.
