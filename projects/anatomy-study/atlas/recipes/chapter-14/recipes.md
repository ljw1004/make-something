# 14. Making room for a breath

1. Lungs + Heart with connected vessels → Heart and lungs — Lungs: transform, Heart with connected vessels: consume.

2. Rib cage + Muscles between the ribs → Breathing apparatus — Rib cage: transform, Muscles between the ribs: consume.

3. Breathing apparatus + Diaphragm → Breathing apparatus [contains Diaphragm] — Breathing apparatus: preserve, Diaphragm: consume.

4. Breathing apparatus + Heart and lungs → Breathing apparatus [contains Diaphragm, Heart and lungs] — Breathing apparatus: preserve, Heart and lungs: consume.
