# 3. Same DNA, different jobs

1. Embryonic cells + Sonic hedgehog protein → Cells with muscle-protein RNA — Embryonic cells: preserve, Sonic hedgehog protein: consume.

2. Cells with muscle-protein RNA + Amino acids — protein building blocks → Muscle cells — Cells with muscle-protein RNA: transform, Amino acids — protein building blocks: consume.

3. Embryonic cells + TGF-beta2 protein → Tendon cells with collagen RNA — Embryonic cells: preserve, TGF-beta2 protein: consume.

4. Tendon cells with collagen RNA + Amino acids — protein building blocks → Tendon tissue — Tendon cells with collagen RNA: transform, Amino acids — protein building blocks: consume.
