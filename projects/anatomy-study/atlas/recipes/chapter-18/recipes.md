# 18. Breaking down a meal

1. Amylase + Salivary fluid → Saliva with amylase — Amylase: consume, Salivary fluid: consume.

2. Starchy food + Saliva with amylase → Shorter starch chains — Starchy food: transform, Saliva with amylase: preserve.

3. Shorter starch chains + Intestinal sugar-digesting enzymes → Meal glucose — Shorter starch chains: transform, Intestinal sugar-digesting enzymes: preserve.

4. Protein food + Proteases → Meal amino acids — Protein food: transform, Proteases: preserve.

5. Fat food + Bile → Small fat droplets — Fat food: transform, Bile: preserve.

6. Small fat droplets + Lipase → Fat digestion products — Small fat droplets: transform, Lipase: preserve.
