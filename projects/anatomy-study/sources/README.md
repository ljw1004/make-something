# Anatomy source library

Retrieved and checked **11–13 November 2026** for private development. The current audience is grades **9–10, non-AP/non-Honors**. [Curriculum grounding and the whole-story audit](../curriculum.md) compare ordinary school expectations and actual story terms; advanced curricula remain clearly labelled comparison sources. The [school scope](school-scope.md) preserves the earlier grades 8–10 prototype boundary. The [broader reading guide and accuracy cautions](reading-guide.md) retain author background, including earlier water-and-heat work. These sources support truthful authoring without making a college anatomy textbook the player's syllabus.

[Scientific grounding across scales](scale-grounding.md) supports exploration of organelles, cells, tissues and systems, including energy, repair and homeostasis. Its [illustration-led revision notes](scale-grounding.md#grounding-for-the-illustration-led-revision) also cover the cell, bone, nerve, pleural and skin relationships used by the current pictures. It adds primary-research checks, model boundaries and optional historical/evolutionary connections for authors; it does not make the college library the player's syllabus.

The local library contains **62 complete OpenStax section texts**, not entire books: 265,874 whitespace-delimited words including attribution, exercises, answers, descriptions and MathML; 1,281 glossary definitions; and text descriptions/captions for 300 figures. The original 25-section library was expanded for the full story with cell synthesis, skin, bone, muscles, nervous and sensory systems, blood, immunity, digestion and circadian background. Copyrighted publisher previews were inspected transiently; only bibliographic information and short original notes are retained here.

The official curricula, standards, course descriptions and advanced comparisons are recorded in [curriculum.md](../curriculum.md#sources-and-exact-scope), with a local manifest under ignored `local/curricula/`. Ordinary-school source additions include NGSS, Pennsylvania Biology, Great Neck's regular ninth-grade course and GCSE specifications, including immune response. Originals and readable Markdown extractions are private research material; the audit retains original findings, precise locations and evidence limits.

### Local files and transformations

All full-text artifacts live in [`local/openstax/`](local/openstax/) and are excluded by this directory's [`.gitignore`](.gitignore). They may be deleted after development; the index and original notes remain useful without them.

- [`original/`](local/openstax/original/) contains the downloaded original **CNXML** modules, the publisher's available historical source format. They are text, not downloaded HTML pages. Text was saved as UTF-8 with normalized line endings.
- [`markdown/`](local/openstax/markdown/) contains the complete selected section text converted to Markdown, with title, attribution, learning objectives, hierarchy, glossary, source questions and answers. Site menus and navigation were never present in these source modules.
- [`html/`](local/openstax/html/) contains **derived text-focused HTML**, not original website HTML. It provides a readable alternative for tables. No image files, video files, logos or covers were downloaded. Figure captions, credit notices and available descriptions are retained; these do not replace visual inspection when anatomy depends on a diagram.
- [`provenance/`](local/openstax/provenance/) retains the publisher license and README, collection metadata, [per-section manifest](local/openstax/provenance/manifest.json) with source URLs/checksums, and the conversion scripts (`convert.py` for the original selection and `convert-full.py` for the expanded collection). Tables retain HTML cell structure inside Markdown. Equations retain a linear text rendering plus their original MathML. Internal cross-references point to pinned publisher source modules; they are not local navigable figure anchors.

The conversion uses Python 3 and `markdownify`; the per-section manifest records word, glossary and figure-description counts and original-source checksums. The 25 original retrieval dates are preserved, and the 37 added modules record 12 November 2026.

The conversion preserves source wording, including errors and simplified explanations. Read the [accuracy cautions](reading-guide.md#accuracy-cautions) before drafting. These files are research material, not a reviewed player script or a substitute for the book's figures.

## Copyrighted references: links and original notes only

### Silverthorn: integrated physiology

Dee Unglaub Silverthorn. *Human Physiology: An Integrated Approach*, 7th edition. Pearson. Student ISBN 978-0-321-98122-6. The official [24-page front matter/preface preview](https://www.pearsoncanada.ca/media/highered-showcase/multi-product-showcase/silverthorn-preface.pdf) was readable through web extraction. Its contents make integration explicit: cellular processes and communication support later work on circulation, breathing, kidneys, metabolism and exercise.

The official [chapter 21 sample, The Digestive System](https://www.pearson.com/content/dam/one-dot-com/one-dot-com/us/en/higher-ed/en/products-services/silverthorn-7e-info/pdf/sample-chapter--ch21.pdf) was actually retrieved (HTTP 200; 39 PDF pages) and text-extracted in memory after the web reader rejected its size. No PDF or full extracted text was retained. It distinguishes motility, secretion, digestion and absorption, then examines coordinated control across digestive phases. **Design inference:** a useful organ assembly should connect a physical structure to several cooperating processes, including a trigger and a response; attaching an organ label is insufficient.

Coverage is these two previews, not the full textbook, later editions or its ancillary resources. No open reproduction license was established.

### Sherwood: homeostasis across systems

## Coverage limits

This is a focused mechanism library, not a complete high-school biology syllabus or full human anatomy collection. It does not systematically cover reproductive development, disease, clinical treatment or every endocrine loop. Immunity, senses and musculoskeletal chapters are selective explanatory cases rather than comprehensive coverage. It has no learning-effectiveness study of the proposed game. The OpenStax material is college introductory level; the [curriculum audit](../curriculum.md) and [design brief](../../../stories/anatomy/design-brief.md) select the current grades 9–10 ordinary-biology teaching boundary. A story about assembling a model must not imply that mature organs are manufactured by simply sticking generic tissues together in real embryological development.
