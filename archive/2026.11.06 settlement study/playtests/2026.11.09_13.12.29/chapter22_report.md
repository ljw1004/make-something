Its chapters moved from stone, brick, food and housing to transport, milling, sanitation, a harbour and shared public spaces. Earlier creations returned in later chapters: seed oil became lamp fuel and soap, while the canopy made for shade in the square became part of the bandstand.

In my chapter, making instruments continued that shift toward community life. I carved wood into a hollow tube and combined it with air to make a flute; stretched cloth over a clay jar to make a drum; and combined a brick-and-plank platform with the earlier canopy to make a bandstand.
