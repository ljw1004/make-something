Nine surviving experiments, from a watershed and an orchard to worlds built by dragging things together.

All were made with Dot in the cloud. This is Lucian’s remembered order, retaining his stage numbers. Dates below identify surviving records, not necessarily the first day of development. All times are Pacific daylight time (UTC−07:00).
