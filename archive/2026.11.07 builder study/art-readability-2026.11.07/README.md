# Readable, connected construction pieces

This revision uses the original [canonical models](../../../archive/2026.11.07%20builder%20study/art/README.md). The [live design brief](../../../stories/builder/design-brief.md) owns the intent and display-size families. Original model dimensions remain unchanged; these adapters assign real components to supplies, select one representative where appropriate, and provide separate loose inspection views.

- `part-presentations.json` explicitly maps every identity to its role, scale family and canonical source. Empty selectors deliberately keep the current geometry. Required replacement masters may not silently fall back to an earlier grouped picture.
- `assembly-splits.json` records the meaningful separate fitting operations. `assembly_views.py` stages their real component subsets and assigns the rover's stationary pen hardware to the prepared deck. Its `FIXED_REGENERATE` list identifies changed construction views.
- `part_views.py` implements explicit component selectors and camera fitting. `part_poses.py` closes real removable covers and coils flexible supplies while preserving line lengths, radii and rigid endpoints. These poses apply only to loose inspections.
- `render_parts.py` renders sharp inspections, registered fixed stages (`--fixed`), and the rover owner plus five matching slot overlays (`--compound`). Use Blender with at most two threads per process. The underside deck and later upright rover are intentionally different assembly stages.
- `scale-policy.json` records the visible-drawing bounds at the 390px reference width. `export_readability.py` exports item images, preserves shared paper transforms, crops fixed successors together, transforms explicit receiving bounds, and rewrites only the manuscript's artwork metadata. The fixed compound uses exactly the same base export as its overlays.
- `paper_views.py` and `render_paper_views.py` render the four picture-strip states in one open-coil inspection pose, preserving paper length, width and printed frame order. `newflight_render.py` and `newflight_compose.py` make labeled detail/setup views from the unchanged flight assemblies. `goal-references.json` maps each construction chapter to its framed visual destination.

The exporter reads original runtime artwork from Git revision `780af8557be813cc83e516e8c243974a0a956df2`, rather than rereading its own promoted output. New PNG masters live temporarily in `runs/` while producing and reviewing a candidate. Final runtime WebPs live beside the story; editable source and concise outcomes are retained without duplicate raster masters. The exporter fails on missing required new images. `--preview` is explicitly incomplete and must never be promoted.

To reproduce a revision, generate both a source catalogue from the baseline manuscript and a current catalogue with the original `catalogue.mjs`. Render the IDs requiring new masters using the current catalogue, `--source-catalogue` pointing to the baseline catalogue, and `--presentations` pointing to this revision's plan. For inspection views pass their IDs to `--ids`; for fixed views use `FIXED_REGENERATE` with `--fixed`; render `rover-deck` separately with `--compound`. Then run `export_readability.py --story CURRENT_MANUSCRIPT --catalogue CURRENT_CATALOGUE --masters INSPECTION_DIRECTORY --fixed-masters FIXED_DIRECTORY --compound-masters COMPOUND_DIRECTORY --output CANDIDATE_DIRECTORY`. Multiple `--masters` directories are applied in order, last replacement winning. The exporter needs Pillow; render scripts run inside Blender.

Supply the shared strip masters with `--paper-masters` and the thirteen composed flight masters with `--flight-masters`. The final screen detail is produced by `newflight_screen.py` and `newflight_screen_compose.py`, using the original reward's actual projection helper. Chapter-specific construction compositions use `CHAPTER--IDENTITY.png` in a `--fixed-masters` directory; they override only that chapter and reserve their full picture, without reusing the previous camera's receiving box. `render_whale_detail_views.py` and `compose_whale_detail_views.py` produce the service/detail views, preserving real occluders and using only the explicitly authored hatch poses. The two inspection chapters preserve their complete 992px square canvases at 248px in play.

[make_whale_detail_queue.py](make_whale_detail_queue.py) deterministically generates the 30 selected main/detail render jobs from [fitted-chapter-views.json](fitted-chapter-views.json). The generator retains the production resolutions (960px main, 640px detail); the configuration retains the cameras, hatch poses and fixed 10W exterior detail light. No pilot queue is required. From this art directory, use the following sequence with absolute catalogue/font paths and disposable output paths under the repository's `runs/` directory:

```sh
python3 make_whale_detail_queue.py --output QUEUE
BLENDER --background --factory-startup --python render_whale_detail_views.py -- --queue QUEUE --catalogue CURRENT_CATALOGUE --source-catalogue BASELINE_CATALOGUE --presentations part-presentations.json --output RAW_DIRECTORY
python3 compose_whale_detail_views.py --source RAW_DIRECTORY --output COMPOSED_DIRECTORY --font SANS_SERIF_FONT
```

Replace `BLENDER` with the local Blender executable and supply `COMPOSED_DIRECTORY` to the exporter's `--fixed-masters`. The queue and raw render manifests can be discarded after production; retain the generator and authoring configuration.

The scale policy reserves at least 44px of canvas in each portable dimension for touch layout, while the visible drawing keeps its own family bounds. Reference cards use the finished or intermediate item images named in the manuscript's `reference:` metadata.

The author audit and goal revision record are separate from independent visual acceptance. See the [revision outcome](../readability-evaluation-2026.11.07.md) for actual tested versions, review coverage and limitations. Temporary manifests, contact sheets, geometry dumps and browser captures are verification inputs, not retained deliverables.
