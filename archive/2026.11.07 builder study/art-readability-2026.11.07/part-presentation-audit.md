# Builder standalone part presentation audit

Author audit, 7 November 2026. The user's new readability and single-connected-item requirements govern this pass; the earlier common-scale presentation and padded 44 px minimum do not establish legibility.

All 340 current standalone item images were inspected on 30 contact sheets at exactly intrinsic dimensions divided by four. The sheets and alpha-bounds inventory are disposable scratch under `runs/builder-readability/`. The audit also consulted the canonical source meshes for candidate selections. It did not use historical review verdicts as current evidence. [part-presentations.json](part-presentations.json) records every original identity and the new split identities, roles, baseline visible bounds, representations and explicit canonical selectors.

A replay of the legal completion route observed 334 identities: 196 movable and 138 only fixed. Six unvisited alternative results belong to fixed receiver chains in their own chapters. The baseline therefore assigns 155 movable parts, 24 intermediates, 17 finished or portable machines, and 144 fixed-only identities. Global hotspot membership alone is insufficient: `whale-running-fair`, `whale-low-tested` and `whale-garden-ready` are movable results before becoming fixed receivers in later chapters. Their standalone icons need only the connected whale; ground equipment remains in hotspot/use views.

The baseline confirms the complaint: 80 primitive drawings have a visible long dimension below 64 CSS px. The test block is about 14 × 15 px, the wave cord about 139 × 3 px, and the dragon spring about 5 × 39 px. Transparent padding improves a target but does not enlarge the drawing. The working scale-family bounds are 80–112 px for loose parts, 112–156 for intermediates and 144–184 for finished toys, measured on visible long dimensions at the 390 px reference width. Thin parts still require a visual check; use a physically faithful compact cord pose or useful inspection angle where needed. Keep animation-strip stages and blank/written banners at matched crops and scales. Fixed assembly stages retain stable registered cameras.

The initial plan supplies 38 explicit canonical selections, two named closed-cover inspection poses, and ten cases requiring an authored resolution. Repeated parts use one complete representative, with quantity and any size variation explained in the supply wording. Installed models keep the full actual set. The selected seven-pilot sheet was also inspected at native contact-sheet size: the axle and its washers are physically continuous, and the bearing, axle unit, dragon frame, shaft, cam and rover read as coherent units. This observation does not establish final-size acceptance.

The ten heterogeneous groups were resolved through [assembly-splits.json](assembly-splits.json): catch apron and scoreboard become separate pinball fittings; wings and pinned tail become actual dragon components; the syringe capture clip and hand holders become distinct operations; the whale gains separate extension, harness, fork, retaining-strap and stop-guide fittings. Jo supplies the fruit-game cup as garden setting while the draggable prop is one actual sponge pear. The rover’s existing stationary cam, guides, spring and stops become prepared deck hardware; the loose pen carriage contains only its connected moving parts. The colour-cell lid and remote cover use real closed-empty inspection poses.

A further source inspection found that the former `lift-posts` icon combined posts, sliding crossbars and nuts. It is now three actual supplies. The crossbars clamp to the lower cradle before it slides over the bare posts; the four top nuts are fitted afterward to capture travel. The resulting manuscript plan has 24 new identities and 12 added operations, for 364 identities and 193 combinations. The final installed components and their dimensions remain unchanged. The misleading ID `lift-supported-module` was also corrected in the selection plan: it means the crane with its attached empty sling, not the separate `balanced-lift` module.

[assembly_views.py](assembly_views.py) implements only visibility and in-memory supply ownership. Its `prepare` method assigns the stationary rover pen mechanism to the deck before registered slot/holdout rendering. Its `stage` method applies explicit component groups after canonical staging. Fixed renders preserve the original camera and never apply a loose representative selection. Thirteen fixed masters and the changed derived icons are explicitly marked for regeneration.

Author verification selected all 37 initially affected identities against the real Blender factories, then checked all seven affected lift identities. Each produced a nonempty selection; before/after world transforms and dimensions were identical. The checks do not prove manufactured connectivity, fit tolerance or final-size readability. Inputs, name inventories and logs remain disposable scratch under `runs/builder-readability/`. No unresolved heterogeneous author decision remains in the presentation plan.

This is an author selection plan, not an independent continuity review, a complete mesh-connectivity proof, or acceptance of the resized catalogue. Fresh review must inspect rendered final-size sheets and representative in-game scenes, checking both visibility and the transition from the selected loose part to its actual fitted counterpart. The source selectors are concrete candidates; failures or newly exposed detached geometry must be corrected before export.
