## Independent visual review

Reviewers were fresh assignments, separate from artwork authors. The manuscript reviewers received the frozen story without author notes. Visual reviewers received exact candidate images and story context at intrinsic size divided by four, plus phone captures or a browser. They were asked to check connected draggable parts, quantity wording, readable scale, actual part-to-assembly continuity, stable camera/material treatment and fitting feedback. Repaired artwork receives a new reviewer. These are informed inspections, not blind recognition rates or human usability tests.

The first exported revision, V24 candidate A, was rejected. Two reviewers inspected all 213 movable pictures and the changed construction sequences. Connectedness passed, but thirteen regenerated fixed views were too dark; long strips, thin parts and late flight framing still needed work. The rendering author traced the lighting failure to stale Blender light transforms and regenerated those views. Compact cord and paper inspection poses, better camera angles, and bounded display families address the remaining reading problems without changing fitted dimensions.
