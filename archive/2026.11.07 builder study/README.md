# Builder study — A Fair in the Air

Completed 7 November 2026. The live [story](../../stories/builder/builder.md), runtime artwork and [single active design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md) now live in `stories/builder`. Future story edits belong there.

The original making story follows a child and Aunt Jo from useful garden toys to a tethered flying fair, with one explicitly fictional lift technology. The first released edition contained 181 authored combinations, 29 playable chapters and an ending. One legal completion used 170 combinations; the other eleven supported alternate experimental orders. The approximate 200-combination request did not justify keeping repetitive fittings that playtests found unrewarding.

The first edition's delivered art comprised 340 item icons, 148 fixed views including a garden-framing variant, five fitted overlays, six page backgrounds, fourteen garden rewards, cover and story icon. There are no copied kit images, layouts, instructions, characters or brand marks. Selected runtime artwork lives only beside the story; editable geometry and render/composition sources are retained here without redundant raster masters.

## 7 November readability and goal-picture revision

The revised live story has **194 combinations**, a **183-combination completion route**, and 29 framed, enlargable pictures of the intended build. Successive goals name concrete next operations. Loose parts, intermediate builds and finished toys use bounded display families; each draggable picture depicts one connected component or one explicitly described representative. Heterogeneous supplies became real separate fittings. Actual service-side and projection details reveal otherwise hidden changes without altering fitted dimensions.

Retained work comprises [v21](builder-v21.md) through [v27](builder-v27.md), the [goal revision record](readability-goals-2026.11.07.md), [evaluation and promotion outcome](readability-evaluation-2026.11.07.md), two additional timestamped playtest batches and [readability artwork sources](art-readability-2026.11.07/README.md). The latter include the selected render requests, camera/pose settings, component selectors and reproducible export/composition code. The live edition contains 366 item images, 168 fixed chapter views and the original five overlays, six backgrounds and fourteen rewards. Historical drafts are source iterations; their original study-relative artwork paths are not additional maintained player routes.

Fresh review rejected dark fixed views, thin inspections, hidden whale fittings and one intermediate-result overlap; the final scoped reviews and browser checks accepted their repairs. The outcome records remaining occlusion, resolution, source-binding and analysis limits. This extends the same study and archive home rather than creating a second project.

Animation was explicitly deferred. The source models retain simple geometry and meaningful pose controls for that later phase. Numerical meshes and finite motion samples do not establish material strength, manufacture or safety; AI chapter trials and informed visual reviews do not establish enjoyment or usability for real children. The authored story remains playable despite the linter's existing exhaustive fixed-state analysis limit.
