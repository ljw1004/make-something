# Contraptions: follow-on development record

Archived on **9 November 2026** when the user abandoned Contraptions. Everything
below records the former Builder study. Proposed next work is closed; the
[archive index](../README.md) and [post-mortem](../2026.11.09-post-mortem.md)
record the final decision.

Develop [The Next Box](../story/contraptions.md) under its
[single design brief](../story/design-brief.md). Earlier work remains
in the [Builder study archive](../README.md).

The live v48 story builds independent paper and wooden toys, moving mechanisms,
optical toys and three final builds with quietly extraordinary pink components. All
workpieces are movable. Literal repeated parts, explicit Fold/Flip/Roll/Punch
processes, short current instructions and titled picture references guide the work
without routine popups. Flat honey wood, lighter green and red, cream and charcoal
sit on white pages; battery cases are blue. Completed whole toys receive short
working loops, and a final silent exchange connects the first pinwheel to another
maker. The [completed revision](next-phase.md) records its blind concept rounds,
mechanical repairs, animation work and acceptance status. A native 3D runtime
remains a separate phase.

The current [worthwhile study](worthwhile-study.md) starts from small ordinary
inventions, with two-to-five-chapter prototypes, casual instruction-following
players and additional questions about what the work actually gives them. It tests
question wording and retains two executable ordinary-invention prototypes: a
[Sweeper](worthwhile/sweeper-v2.md) and [snack Carrier](worthwhile/carrier-v2.md).
Twenty chapter sessions, including wording pilots and a Creation control, found
specific assembly payoffs but did not establish sustained interest. The study
records revisions, prompt sensitivity, score tables and the untested drawing-engine
hypothesis; no new visual production candidate has been accepted. Earlier
[narrative redevelopment](narrative-redevelopment.md) and the
[Helping Hand proposal](story-treatment.md) are set aside; they did not establish
that the underlying construction experience was worth sustaining.

Experience hypothesis: following the goal picture and one short current instruction
lets the player see and understand every physical change. Reject fitted parts that
cannot be distinguished, processes that silently add hardware, attachments that
deform unrelated components, or clutter that makes the next piece inaccessible.
Some recoverable tabletop overlap is intentional.

The [preceding full revision record](full-story-evaluation.md) preserves review instructions,
rejected candidates, accepted outcomes and evidence limits. Earlier
[pinwheel](chapter-one-evaluation.md) and [racer](racer-evaluation.md) records remain
attributed to their own versions. Numbered drafts and actual structured playtests
are retained. The current promoted candidate is [v48](builder-v48.md), with 216
construction actions, 521 recipes, 32 playable chapters and an ending. The prior
full revision remains [v40](builder-v40.md). Draft artwork references point to promoted live assets and are not
immutable visual snapshots.

Production sources are in `chapter-one-art/`, `racer-art/` and
[full-story/](full-story/README.md), sharing `materials.py`. Canonical dimensioned
Blender geometry supplies all related stages and the [working loops](animation/README.md).
`full-story/captions.json` contains picture descriptions audited against each
selected artwork revision: `{...}` passages appear in text
players and are hidden in the web game. These verbal proxies do not establish
visual enjoyment or native touch quality.

Selected runtime artwork has one home under `stories/builder/assets/`. The
[selected-art record](runtime-art.json) binds promoted images to source hashes and
render passes. Temporary renders, captures and reviewer inputs belong in ignored
`runs/`; retain only concise outcomes and the standing playtest exceptions.
