# Builder: working toys and an unspoken connection

Completed revision under the [single brief](../story/design-brief.md).
The chronological outcomes below preserve rejected attempts as well as accepted
repairs. The final acceptance section identifies the promoted candidate.

## Work and acceptance

1. Compare three different visible late sequences with fresh blind readers. Develop
   three variants of the leading direction and repeat for at least three rounds.
   Readers receive only the current visible sequence, no intended hidden premise,
   history or earlier reviews. Retain concise outcomes here; scratch packets go in runs.
2. Cut two weaker early whole builds; retain a coherent progression into three
   substantial final builds with pink modules and an observable emotional payoff.
3. Center single processes, show chapter/reference titles, tighten each current
   crop, keep all touch targets usable, expose every attachment and match loose
   component orientation. Give all battery cases one distinct blue material.
4. Produce 5–10-second working loops for completed whole objects from canonical
   Blender models; review geometry, motion, continuity and actual displayed size.
5. Audit literal picture descriptions, run fresh fast/detailed playtests, check
   complete legal routes and browser drags/reload/reduced motion. Iterate findings.
6. Promote accepted source/art/story, build, commit and push; retain concise evidence
   and actual structured playtest records under the existing retention policy.
